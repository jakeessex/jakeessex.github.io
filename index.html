About

Jake Essex

Jake Essex is a singer from Essex. He’s 28 and has been singing for a decade, all across the UK. Classic 50s and 60s rock ’n’ roll — Elvis-inspired, but he is not an impersonator and not a tribute. He is himself.

High energy. Continuous flow. The room does not get a gap to go cold.

His nan put that music on in the house. It stuck.

The set draws on Elvis, Bobby Darin, Dion, Buddy Holly, Roy Orbison, Jerry Lee Lewis, Willie Nelson, Frank Sinatra, and that era.
