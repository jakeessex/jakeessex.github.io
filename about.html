About

Jake Essex

A singer from Essex. High energy. Continuous flow. No dead air.

Not an impersonator. Not a tribute act.
He performs as himself — drawing on Elvis, Bobby Darin, Dion, Buddy Holly, Roy Orbison, Jerry Lee Lewis, Willie Nelson, Frank Sinatra and the rest of that era.

By day he’s an RAC mobile mechanic.
By night he’s on stage delivering one of the strongest rock ’n’ roll shows in the East of England.

The show has a clear shape.
It starts with the era.
It finishes with Elvis.
And the room leaves knowing they’ve seen something different.
