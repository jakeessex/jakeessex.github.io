About

Jake Essex

A singer from Essex. Elvis-inspired, not an impersonator. He sings as himself.

He draws on Elvis, Bobby Darin, Dion, Buddy Holly, Roy Orbison, Jerry Lee Lewis and Frank Sinatra.

By day he is an RAC mobile mechanic. By night he is on stage with one of the strongest rock 'n' roll shows in the East of England.

The show has a clear shape. It starts with the era. It finishes with Elvis.
