FAQ

Do you do a pure Elvis tribute?

No. I perform as myself. The show uses Elvis material — especially later in the night — but it is not an impersonation show.

Do you bring a PA?

Yes. Fully self-contained. You don’t need to hire anything.

Are you insured?

Yes. £10 million public liability, and the kit is PAT tested. Invoices come from Jake Essex Music Ltd.

How far do you travel?

Essex, London, Kent and the East of England as standard. West Midlands and further afield by arrangement — just ask.

Can we request songs?

Yes, within the era. Send a few titles with the booking. The night still keeps its shape: the era, then Elvis, finishing on The Impossible Dream.

How do I book?

Send the date, approximate start time, venue and which show you want. I’ll confirm availability on the same email.
